How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a classic 3x3 grid strategy game where two players compete to form a line of three matching symbols horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

  • Player 1 (X): Cyan neon cross symbol.
  • Player 2 / AI (O): Pink neon circle symbol.
  • Winning Condition: Align 3 of your marks in a straight row before your opponent blocks you!
